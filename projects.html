Asalaamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah

A message from Kerry Islamic Cultural Centre

All praise is due to Allah (سبحانہ وتعالی). Alhamdulillah, work on our new site at Oakpark, Tralee is well underway — the community centre exterior is taking shape and we are already using parts of the building for Madrasa and community activities.

However, the main masjid prayer hall has not yet been constructed. We still need your generous support to:

  • Build the main masjid for daily salah and Jumu’ah
  • Complete outstanding community centre costs
  • Finalise the car park and site works
  • Repay Qardh Hasanah (interest-free loans from the community)

This is Lillah — charity that continues to benefit you long after you have left this world. Every prayer, every Jumu’ah, every child learning Qur’an within these walls can add to your scale of good deeds, in sha Allah.

“Spend in charity, O son of Adam, and I will spend on you.” — Hadith Qudsi

In Progress

Community Centre

The two-storey community building is being constructed. The exterior and shell are visible on site — this is where Madrasa, meetings, and community activities are already beginning to take place.

Your support helps: complete remaining build costs, fit-out, and car park works.

Planned

Main Masjid

The dedicated prayer hall — the heart of the project — is not yet built. Our architectural plans show a beautiful masjid that will serve Muslims across Kerry for generations.

Your support helps: fund the construction of the main masjid prayer hall and wudhu facilities.
