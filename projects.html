A message from Kerry Islamic Cultural Centre

All praise is due to Allah (سبحانہ وتعالی), the Most Kind, the Most Merciful. Alhamdulillah, our new masjid in Tralee is complete and we have moved in. Yet we urgently need your support to finalise the remaining costs and repay Qardh Hasanah.

This is not simply a building project. This is Lillah — a charity that continues to benefit you and reward you long after you have left this world. Every prayer, every Jumu'ah, every child learning Qur'an within these walls can add to your scale of good deeds, in sha Allah, for years and generations to come.

"Spend in charity, O son of Adam, and I will spend on you." — Hadith Qudsi
