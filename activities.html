How to Join

Everyone is welcome. Most programmes are free; weekly classes are usually drop-in.

  1. 1

    Pick a programme

    Check this week’s schedule or browse the programme guide.

  2. 2

    Come along

    Turn up at the masjid for weekly classes. For madrasa or special events, contact us first.

  3. 3

    Stay in the loop

    Schedule changes are posted on the homepage notice board. For email updates, join the Kerry Muslim database.
