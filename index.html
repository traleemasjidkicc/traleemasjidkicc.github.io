Our community

Welcome to Tralee Masjid

Since 1988, Kerry Islamic Cultural Centre has served Muslims across Co. Kerry with daily salah, education, and community support — rooted in simplicity, sincerity, and service to Allah.

Whether you are visiting for Jumu’ah, enrolling a child in madrasa, or seeking a welcoming Muslim community in the south-west of Ireland, you are welcome here.

Belief

Pillars of Faith

The six articles of Iman — the foundation of every Muslim’s belief. Select each pillar to explore what we hold true.

Oneness of God Tawheed

Allah alone is worthy of worship

We believe that God exists and that He is the Master and Lord of the universe and the only One deserving of worship, devotion and ultimate obedience. “There is no true God worthy of worship but God and Muhammad is the last and final Messenger of God.”

Angels Mala’ika

Noble servants of Allah, unseen yet real

We believe that angels exist, believe in the names we are informed of and of their descriptions in authentic reports. They are created by God and have no share in divinity. Their purpose is to serve God and to carry out His commands in the universe.

Revealed Books Kutub

Divine guidance sent to humanity

We believe that God sent revelations and communicated with His chosen messengers. We believe in the names of the original scriptures and believe in the truth contained therein which is confirmed by the Qur’an.

Prophets Rusul

Messengers who called mankind to Allah

We believe that God sent human Messengers to remind people to worship God alone on His terms and to obey His commandments. They promised reward for those who comply and do good and warned of a punishment for those who do evil.

Day of Judgement Akhirah

Accountability before Allah after death

We believe that we will be brought back to life after death to be judged by God. Paradise and Hell are the eternal destination of people based on how they lived their lives and the choices they made.

Divine Decree Qadar

Allah’s perfect knowledge and plan

We believe that God knows everything that is to come and He recorded everything in a preserved tablet before the creation came into existence.

Practice

Pillars of Islam

Five acts that uphold a Muslim’s way of life. Hover or tap each pillar to learn more.

Testifying that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His final Messenger — the gateway through which every act of worship becomes meaningful.

Five daily prayers anchor the believer’s day in remembrance of Allah, build discipline and humility, and unite the community at the masjid.

Fasting in Ramadan cultivates taqwa, self-restraint, and empathy for those who go hungry — a month of spiritual renewal for the whole ummah.

Obligatory charity purifies wealth, supports those in need, and strengthens bonds of care and responsibility within the Muslim community.

The journey to Makkah once in a lifetime for those who are able — a profound act of submission, equality, and unity with Muslims from every corner of the world.
