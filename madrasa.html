Why Choose Our Madrasa?

A caring, structured environment where children grow in faith, manners, and confidence — with you as our partner every step of the way.

Faith That Lasts

Children learn Qur’an recitation, duas, and the essentials of Islam in a way that is engaging, age-appropriate, and rooted in authentic sources.

Safe & Nurturing

Classes run in the masjid with qualified teachers, clear behaviour expectations, and a welcoming atmosphere where every child is known and valued.

We Work With You

We recognise that home and Madrasa together shape a child’s character. We keep communication open so you always know how your child is progressing.

Enrolling Is Simple

Three easy steps — most families complete registration in a single WhatsApp conversation.

  1. 1

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us your child’s name, age, and whether you need a girls’ or boys’ class. Imam Mustafa or the Madrasa team will reply promptly.

  2. 2

    We confirm your session

    We match your child to the right class time from our weekday schedule and answer any questions about what to expect.

  3. 3

    Attend the first lesson

    Bring your child to Kerry Islamic Cultural Centre at the agreed time. Teachers will welcome them and guide them from day one.

Our Teaching Programme

Built on the An Nasihah Curriculum

We follow the widely trusted An Nasihah Islamic Curriculum — a structured, child-friendly programme spanning eight years, designed for young Muslims today.

  • Colourful, illustrated course books that make learning enjoyable
  • Content verified by senior scholars using authentic sources
  • All key subjects in one coursebook: Fiqh, Hadith, Sirah, History, Aqidah, Akhlaq & Adab
  • Matching workbooks to reinforce learning at home

A Note for Parents

We aim to provide knowledge in a caring Islamic environment, to nurture excellent manners, and to prepare future leaders of our community — with your support at home.

Guidelines for Pupils

  • Please ensure children complete wudhu at home before attending. Toilets and the wudhu area are for emergency use only during class time.
  • If your child is unwell, has a fever, or flu-like symptoms, please keep them at home until they are fully recovered.
  • If your child has a medical condition or allergy, inform the Imam or teacher as soon as possible.
  • Hand sanitiser is available in the Madrasa.
  • Parents dropping off or collecting children should avoid gathering in the masjid car park.
